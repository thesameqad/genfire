How do I play on a phone?

The stick at the bottom left moves your hero. Touch anywhere else to aim and fire. Your shots lock on to monsters near your finger.

Can I see what the aim is locking on to?

Yes. Turn on Auto-Aim Outline in Settings or in the pause menu.

Why are Normal and Hard locked?

They open once you finish the game on Easy.
