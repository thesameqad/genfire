GenFire Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. GenFire is made by Erman Games.

What stays on your device

Your progress, upgrades and settings are saved on your device only. GenFire has no accounts and no advertising.

What is collected

GenFire sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics: which levels are started, finished, failed or left, the difficulty, coins, monsters defeated, deaths, time played, upgrades bought, the game version and the type of device. They are linked to a random ID that the game creates on first launch. They do not include your name, contact details or any advertising ID.

On computers, GenFire also collects data if you choose to add a score to the leaderboard. The name you type, your score and the time are sent to a Google Firebase database and shown to all players. Please use a nickname, not your real name. The iPhone and iPad versions have no leaderboard.

How it is used

Gameplay statistics are used only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. Leaderboard data is used only to show the leaderboard. This data is not sold, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Google processes it on our behalf, under its own privacy policy and Firebase privacy terms.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Gameplay statistics are kept according to the retention settings in Google Analytics, and scores stay on the leaderboard until they are removed. To have a score removed, or to ask anything about privacy, use the contact on the support page. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device, including the random ID.

Children

GenFire does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child has entered personal information as a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
