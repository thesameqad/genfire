GenFire Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. GenFire is made by Erman Games.

What stays on your device

Your progress, upgrades and settings are saved on your device only. GenFire has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What is collected

GenFire collects data only if you choose to add a score to the leaderboard. The name you type, your score and the time are sent to a Google Firebase database and shown to all players. Nothing else is sent, and the game asks for no other personal information. Please use a nickname, not your real name.

How it is used

This data is used only to show the leaderboard. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else. Google hosts the database on our behalf, under its own Firebase privacy terms.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Scores stay on the leaderboard until they are removed. To have a score removed, or to ask anything about privacy, use the contact on the support page. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device.

Children

GenFire does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child has entered personal information as a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
